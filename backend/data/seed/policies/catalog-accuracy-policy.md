# Catalog Accuracy & Listing Policy

This policy governs what the product page promised, separately from what it cost. A customer complaining that a product does not match its description, images or specifications has a complaint about us, not about the product itself, and it needs an owner even when nothing is technically defective.

## 1. Description, Image and Specification Mismatch

Where a product received does not match its listing's description, primary images, or stated specifications in a way that would reasonably have changed the customer's decision to buy — wrong colour actually shipped and shown, a feature described that the unit does not have, dimensions materially different from stated — this is a "not as described" case and is remedied under §6, not treated as a defect requiring a warranty assessment. A minor, disclosed variation (packaging design updates, a stated "actual colour may vary slightly") is not a mismatch and is explained rather than treated as a complaint failure.

## 2. Missing Compatibility Information

Where a customer bought an accessory or spare part based on the listing's stated compatibility and it does not fit or work with their product, and the listing's compatibility information was incomplete, wrong, or absent, this is a listing defect and the remedy in §6 applies regardless of the item's condition or whether it was opened to test the fit. Where the listing's compatibility information was correct and complete and the customer simply ordered against the wrong model, standard return handling under returns §1 applies instead.

## 3. Stale or Incorrect Price Display

Where a listing shows a price that has not updated correctly — a display bug rather than a live, intended price — this is a catalog defect to fix immediately, but the customer's entitlement to what they saw and paid for is governed by sales §2, not by this policy. Report the stale display for correction the same day it is found, whether or not it resulted in a complaint.

## 4. Mandatory Listing Disclosures

Every listing must display the Maximum Retail Price, net quantity, country of origin, manufacturer or packer details, and consumer-care contact information, as required under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules and applicable e-commerce disclosure requirements. Where any of these is missing or wrong on a listing a customer asks about, treat it as a compliance defect and escalate for correction ahead of routine catalog fixes — this is not a cosmetic issue, and MRP specifically ties directly to sales §1.

## 5. Correction Turnaround and Listing Pull

A confirmed catalog defect affecting active orders (a wrong price, a materially wrong description or image) is corrected within **1 business day** of being confirmed. Where a defect cannot be corrected that quickly and is actively misleading customers into a purchase they would not otherwise make, the listing is pulled from sale until it is fixed — losing sales on one SKU for a day costs less than the complaints and corrections that follow from leaving it live.

## 6. Remedy for "Not as Described" and Return Shipping

A confirmed catalog mismatch under §1 or §2 is always treated as company error: the customer may return the item for a full refund or exchange, reverse pickup is arranged at no cost regardless of the item's opened or used condition (within reason — genuinely damaged-by-use items are assessed individually), and any shipping originally charged is refunded in full, consistent with returns §4's company-error carve-out. The customer is never asked to bear the cost of a mistake in what we told them the product was.
