# Production & Manufacturing Defect Policy

This policy governs defects traceable to how a unit was made — across our own production and our contract manufacturers — and the containment action taken once a production cause is established. The customer-facing remedy comes from warranty or product_safety; what follows here is about stopping the next hundred units from reaching a customer at all.

## 1. Traceability Is the Precondition

A production defect cannot be acted on without knowing what produced it. Every claim routed here must carry the serial number, the batch or lot identifier, and where available the date code — from which the production line and contract manufacturer are recoverable. Where a claim arrives without traceability, recover it from fulfilment-centre inbound records before closing the ticket rather than after (qa §2).

## 2. Tooling and Process Defects

A defect that appears in the same physical location on multiple units — a housing cracking along one seam, a fastening failing at one boss, a finish flaw in one region — is a process signature, not coincidence, and is presumed to be a manufacturing fault once the pattern is confirmed on a second unit from the same batch. Mould misalignment, worn tooling, incorrect torque settings and cure-time deviations all produce this shape of evidence. A presumed manufacturing fault is never charged to the customer, and never characterised to them as mishandling or transit damage where the pattern says otherwise.

## 3. Quarantine Authority

Manufacturing may place a batch on quarantine immediately on a confirmed defect pattern, without waiting for root cause: a quarantine is reversible and a shipped defect is not. A quarantine stops all outbound movement of that batch from every fulfilment centre — new orders, warranty replacement stock, and exchange stock alike — and the stock is physically segregated in the warehouse management system so it cannot be picked or auto-allocated to an order. The quarantine is recorded with the batch identifier, the defect, and the named person who placed it. Quarantined stock is never used to fulfil a replacement for the very defect that caused the quarantine (qa §4).

## 4. Containment Is Not a Recall

Quarantining a batch, inspecting stock, and holding inventory out of sale are internal actions we take on our own goods and may take at any time. A recall is a public act with regulatory consequences, and the decision belongs jointly to Product Safety and Legal under product_safety §3.1 — manufacturing does not make it, announce it, or hint at it. Where a containment action touches units already with customers, the decision about whether and how to contact them also sits with Product Safety and Legal, not here. Staff must not describe a quarantine as a recall internally either, because internal language leaks into customer-facing text.

## 5. Contract Manufacturer and Component Nonconformance

Where the cause is a contract manufacturer's process or an incoming component rather than our own design, the defect is still ours to remedy with the customer, immediately and without reference to the manufacturer's or supplier's role. In parallel, the nonconformance is raised with them, the affected production run is identified, and the cost of the remedy is recovered under the manufacturing agreement. Contract manufacturer names, lot numbers and commercial terms are internal and never appear in customer-facing text (comms §3). A change of contract manufacturer or component supplier is itself a defect signal worth watching for a period afterwards (qa §1).

## 6. Verified-Good Replacement Stock

Where a batch defect requires replacing units already sold — to consumers under warranty, or to a retail, wholesale or marketplace partner under partner §4 — the replacement is drawn only from stock explicitly verified to be outside the affected batch, never from unverified inventory of the same SKU. A customer or partner discovering the same defect twice costs us the relationship, not just the units.

## 7. Corrective Action and Release

A batch comes off quarantine only when the cause is corrected, the correction is verified on production output rather than on a sample of intent, and the verification is recorded against the original defect. Where qa opened an investigation for the same defect, that investigation closes on this verification and not before (qa §5). If a corrective action changes what the product does or how it should be handled, the affected policies are revised under kb §5 — a fix that nobody downstream knows about will be re-diagnosed as a defect for years.
