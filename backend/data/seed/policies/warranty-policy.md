# Product Warranty Policy

This policy governs warranty coverage, claims, and remedies for all products sold directly through our website and app, or through authorized retail and marketplace partners.

## 1. Coverage Period

All products carry a standard 12-month limited warranty from the original date of purchase, verified by order ID or registered serial number. Units sold through a retail or marketplace partner follow the same 12-month window from the partner's invoice date, not the end-customer's purchase date, unless a separate partner agreement states otherwise.

## 2. Manufacturing Defects

### 2.3 Structural and Component Failures

Cracks, breakage, or failure of structural components (housings, blade assemblies, latches, charging circuits) under normal use, without evidence of drop damage, misuse, or unauthorized modification, are presumed to be manufacturing defects and are covered for repair or replacement at the company's discretion. Support staff should default to replacement for components under ₹4,000 in part cost, since the administrative cost of routing the unit through service typically exceeds the part value.

## 3. Accidental Damage (Exclusions)

Warranty does not cover damage from drops, liquid exposure beyond normal splash resistance, unauthorized disassembly, use with non-approved accessories, or damage occurring after a warranty claim has already been fulfilled once for the same unit. Accidental damage claims should be routed to the optional AccidentGuard add-on plan if the customer purchased it; otherwise, offer a discounted spare-parts order (spare_parts §4).

## 4. Extended Warranty & Registration

### 4.1 Gifted or Transferred Products

Products registered under the CarePlan+ program extend standard warranty to 24 months. Registration transfers with the product — a gifted or resold item retains its registered warranty status as long as the serial number was registered by the original purchaser, regardless of who is now filing the claim or whether they hold the original receipt. Serial number lookup is sufficient proof of purchase; a receipt or original order ID should never be a hard requirement for warranty service.

## 5. Safety Exceptions

### 5.1 Immediate Remedy for Reported Hazards

Any warranty claim that includes a reported safety hazard — smoke, burning smell, sparking, overheating beyond normal operating temperature, or unexpected mechanical failure during use — bypasses the standard repair-first remedy hierarchy. These claims are authorized for an immediate full refund or immediate replacement at the customer's choice, regardless of the unit's age within the warranty window or whether repair would normally be attempted first. Do not ask the customer to continue using or testing a unit that has exhibited a safety symptom. All safety-exception claims must be logged for the quality escalation queue (see product_safety policy §1) in addition to the warranty resolution.

## 6. Claim Process

Warranty claims are validated using order ID or registered serial number. No physical inspection is required before issuing a remedy for claims under ₹12,000 in replacement value; higher-value claims may request photos or a technician assessment (§7) but should not delay the customer's remedy by more than one business day while they are reviewed.

## 7. Service Delivery

A confirmed defect is remedied through one of four routes, in this order of preference, chosen by what the product and the fault actually require rather than by what is administratively easiest for us:

1. **On-site service visit** — for large appliances and anything impractical to ship, a technician from the authorized service network visits the customer's registered address.
2. **Walk-in service centre** — for customers within reasonable distance of a centre who prefer not to wait for a visit slot.
3. **Ship-in repair** — a prepaid pickup of the unit for repair at a service facility, used where no on-site or walk-in option covers the customer's pincode.
4. **Replacement or refund** — used where the fault is not economically repairable, where §2.3 already directs replacement, or where none of the above routes can reach the customer inside the target in §8.

A customer is never required to attempt a route higher on this list than the fault warrants: a dead charging circuit under §2.3 goes straight to replacement, it does not wait for a diagnostic visit first.

## 8. Service Turnaround and Pincode Coverage

An on-site visit is scheduled within 2 business days of the fault being confirmed as warranty-covered, in serviceable pincodes. Where the registered address falls outside the authorized service network's coverage, the customer is told this plainly on first contact — not discovered after a visit is booked and missed — and moved directly to ship-in repair or replacement, whichever the fault under §7 warrants, at no extra cost or delay penalty to the customer for living outside coverage. A repair that cannot be completed within 10 business days of the unit reaching the service centre is escalated to a replacement automatically, without the customer having to ask.

## 9. Out-of-Warranty Paid Service

Once the coverage period in §1 has passed, a service visit or repair is available on a paid basis at the authorized service network's standard rates, quoted before the visit is confirmed. Where the technician's assessment finds the fault was in fact covered under §2 — a manufacturing defect rather than wear or misuse — the visit is retrospectively free and any diagnostic charge already collected is refunded in full. A paid out-of-warranty repair never overrides a live safety exception under §5.1: a reported hazard is remedied under §5.1 regardless of warranty status, and product_safety §4 applies in parallel.
