# Statutory Grievance Redressal Policy

This policy is company-wide and states the statutory floor: what consumer-protection law requires of us for handling complaints, independent of anything else in this corpus. These obligations are set externally — under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules and the Consumer Protection Act 2019 — and this policy is re-verified against the current legal text at every review (kb §5), not amended from memory.

## 1. The Grievance Officer

We designate a named Grievance Officer, whose name and contact details (email, phone, and postal address) are published on our website and app. The Grievance Officer's role is to receive, acknowledge and address consumer grievances that reach that channel specifically, and to ensure the timelines in §2 are met. Where a customer explicitly asks to escalate to the Grievance Officer rather than continuing through ordinary support, that request is honoured and routed there without being deflected back into the standard queue.

## 2. Acknowledgement and Redressal Timelines

Every grievance is acknowledged within **48 hours** of receipt and redressed within **one month** of receipt, as required by law. These are the statutory floor, not our target — our own commitments in sla §2 and §3 are tighter in every case, and this policy exists so the legal minimum is met even in the rare case something falls outside the ordinary queue's targets, not as the standard we design around. Where a complaint genuinely cannot be resolved within one month, the customer is told why and given a specific revised date before the month elapses, mirroring the breach obligation in sla §4.

## 3. National Consumer Helpline (NCH/INGRAM) Convergence

Complaints reaching us through the National Consumer Helpline's grievance convergence system (INGRAM) are treated as grievances under this policy from the moment they arrive, regardless of how routine the underlying issue is — the fact that a customer went to the Helpline rather than to us directly is itself a signal the ordinary channel did not serve them, and the response is handled with the same escalation attention as one arriving through legal §1.

## 4. Internal Escalation Path

A grievance that cannot be resolved by the agent or lead handling it within the normal escalation route (escalation §6) is escalated to the Grievance Officer directly rather than continuing to circulate within the support queue. The Grievance Officer has authority to direct a resolution consistent with this corpus, or to route the matter to Legal where it meets a trigger under legal §1.

## 5. Record-Keeping Obligations

Every grievance handled under this policy — its receipt date, acknowledgement date, resolution date, and outcome — is retained for the period required by applicable regulation, which is at least as long as the general ticket retention in privacy §5 and is not shortened by it where the two differ. This record is what allows us to demonstrate compliance with §2 if ever asked, and is not optional documentation.

## 6. Escalation to Consumer Commissions

Where a customer is not satisfied with our resolution and takes the complaint to a District, State or National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, the matter moves entirely to Legal under legal §1 and §7, and support's role narrows to the single-channel handling in legal §5. Support never advises a customer on whether to escalate to a Commission, how to file, or what outcome to expect — that is outside our role entirely, and a customer asking is told they are free to do so and given the Grievance Officer's details as the first step.

## 7. Relationship to Our Own Targets

Nothing in this policy is meant to be the standard a customer experiences day to day — it is the backstop that applies if our own targets in sla §2 and §3 are somehow missed. An agent who cites this policy's timelines to a customer instead of resolving their complaint promptly has misunderstood its purpose; it exists to protect the customer when everything else has failed, not to define what "fast enough" means for a working queue.
