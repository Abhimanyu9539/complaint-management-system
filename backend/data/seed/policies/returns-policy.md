# Returns Policy

This policy governs eligibility, condition and refund method for customer-initiated returns — reasons other than defect or damage (see warranty and shipping policies for those cases). How the pickup itself is scheduled and executed, and exactly when the refund is released, is covered in the reverse-logistics policy.

## 1. Standard Return Window

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unopened or in like-new condition with original packaging. The 30-day clock starts from the delivery date on record, not the order date.

## 2. Condition Requirements

Unopened items in original packaging qualify for full refund. Opened but unused items with all original packaging and accessories qualify for a full refund at agent discretion, or a 10% restocking consideration if packaging is materially damaged or incomplete. Used items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns; direct these to the warranty or tech_support policies if a functional issue is involved.

## 3. Exceptions & Courtesy Extensions

Requests to return items slightly past the 30-day window (a "reasonable" grace period is up to 10 days past) may be approved as a one-time courtesy, particularly for customers with an established order history or a documented reason (travel, hospitalization) for the delay. This is an exception, not a policy change — agents should note in the ticket that a courtesy exception was applied so repeat late-return requests from the same account can be reviewed differently.

## 4. Refund Method

Refunds for standard returns go to the original payment method. If the original outbound shipping was a paid (non-free-shipping-threshold) option, that shipping fee is deducted from the refund unless the return is due to company error (wrong item shipped, damaged, defective) — in those cases the full amount including original shipping is refunded.

## 5. Non-Returnable Items

Spare parts ordered individually (see spare_parts handling), gift cards, and clearance/final-sale items are not eligible for standard returns. Defect or safety issues with non-returnable items are still handled under the warranty or product_safety policies, which take precedence over the non-returnable designation.
