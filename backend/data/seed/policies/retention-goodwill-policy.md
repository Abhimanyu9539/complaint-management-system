# Retention, Goodwill & Compensation Policy

This policy governs discretionary spend: the credits, vouchers and discounts we offer beyond what a customer is owed, and the offers we make to keep a customer who is leaving. It is the company's authority on goodwill values — every other policy that mentions goodwill points here.

## 1. Goodwill Is Not a Refund

A refund returns money the customer paid for something they did not get — it is an entitlement, it is documented by the charge itself, and its authority sits in billing §1. Goodwill is discretionary spend that acknowledges the cost of the experience: the wasted evening, the third phone call, the delivery that ruined a plan. The two are issued separately, recorded separately, and never substituted for each other. A customer owed a refund cannot be given wallet credit instead unless they choose it (stored_value §5), and a customer who received a full refund may still be owed goodwill for what the failure cost them.

## 2. Goodwill Authority

| Value | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹4,000 | The agent handling the ticket, no approval needed |
| ₹4,001 – ₹15,000 | A lead, recorded on the ticket before it is issued |
| Above ₹15,000 | Admin, recorded on the ticket before it is issued |

One goodwill gesture per ticket: if the situation warrants more than was first given, raise the single gesture rather than stacking a second one, so the total is visible in one place. Wallet credit is preferred over a card refund where the customer is staying with us, because it is worth more to them at the same cost to us (stored_value §1) — but a customer who asks for the money back and has been through a genuine failure gets the refund. The value is chosen by what the failure cost the customer, not by how forcefully they complained; matching generosity to volume teaches customers to escalate.

## 3. When Goodwill Is Required

Some failures are ours by definition and do not wait for the customer to ask:

- **A third contact on one unresolved issue** — tech_support §3.
- **A missed response or resolution target** — sla §4.
- **A repeat of the same failure after we told the customer it was fixed**, including a replacement that failed the same way.
- **A remedy we promised and did not deliver** on the date we gave.

In these cases the gesture is offered in the same message that explains the failure, not held back for a customer who pushes.

## 4. When Goodwill Is Forbidden

Goodwill is withheld in two situations, both absolute:

- **A live legal or injury claim** — legal §3. An apology credit on an injury report reads as settlement, and support does not settle.
- **An account under abuse review** — fraud §3. Discretionary spend pauses; entitlements do not.

In both cases the customer is not told they are ineligible for goodwill — they are told who is now handling their case, or simply given the entitlement they are owed without the discretionary extra.

## 5. Save Offers and Loyalty Discounts

A customer cancelling CarePlan+ or another subscription over price may be offered a discount rather than losing the relationship, scaled by how long they have been with us:

- **Accounts of 6 months or more** — the previous rate may be reinstated for up to 12 months (for example, a ₹749/month plan restored from a ₹999/month renewal price).
- **Accounts of 12 months or more** — the previous rate for up to 12 months, plus one service tier or add-on at no charge for the same period.
- **Accounts under 6 months** — no rate reinstatement; address the reason for leaving instead, since a discount this early usually means we sold the wrong plan.

A save offer is made once. It is not repeated at each renewal, and a customer who accepted one and cancels again a year later is not offered a deeper one automatically. Where the cancellation reason is a product failure rather than price, fix the failure first — a discount offered instead of a resolution reads as a bribe.

## 6. Cancellation Must Not Be Obstructed

If the customer declines the offer, the cancellation is processed on that contact. One offer, then done: no second attempt, no transfer to another agent to try again, no requirement to call when they asked in writing, and no delay that lets another billing cycle land. The effective date is the date the customer asked, not the date we processed it, per billing §2 — and any charge that lands after that date because of our processing delay is refunded in full. Making cancellation difficult costs more in complaints and chargebacks than the retained revenue is worth.

## 7. Record the Reason

Every save attempt records the stated reason for leaving and whether the offer was accepted, because the aggregate is the most honest product feedback the company receives. A cancellation traced to a product fault, a billing failure or a support experience is routed to the owning department as a complaint in its own right, not filed as churn — retention is where the company finds out what is broken, and treating it as a discount desk wastes that.
