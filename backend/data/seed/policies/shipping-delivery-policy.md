# Shipping & Delivery Policy

This policy governs delivery timing, lost packages, transit damage, and expedited remedies — whether a shipment arrived, arrived late, or arrived damaged. Wrong item, missing item, partial delivery, a tampered parcel, or a failed delivery attempt are accuracy and access failures and are handled under delivery §1–§5 instead.

## 1. Delivery Windows

Standard shipping delivers within 5–7 business days; express within 1–2 business days. A shipment is not considered "late" for support purposes until it exceeds the standard window by more than 2 business days, at which point proactive tracking review is appropriate even before the customer contacts support.

## 2. Lost Package Claims

If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer states the package was not received, a carrier claim should be filed immediately rather than waiting out an internal investigation window. In cases where the delivery address has passive package logging (a residential society's security desk, a building watchman's register, a mailroom, or a smart-lockbox with photo confirmation) and no corresponding log entry exists, this is treated as presumptive carrier loss — the customer should not be required to wait for the carrier's claim outcome before receiving a replacement or refund.

## 3. Damaged in Transit

### 3.1 Write-Off Threshold

For units under ₹12,000 in replacement value, photo evidence of transit damage (crushed packaging, visible external damage, or a description of internal rattling consistent with impact) is sufficient to approve a replacement without requiring the customer to return the damaged unit first. Units at or above ₹12,000 typically require a reverse pickup of the damaged unit (reverse §1) before replacement ships, unless the damage poses a safety risk (see product_safety policy §4), in which case the write-off threshold does not apply and immediate replacement is authorized regardless of value.

## 4. Expedited Replacement Eligibility

Customers who state a time-sensitive reason (upcoming move, event, medical need) for a damaged or lost shipment are eligible for expedited (express or same-day where available) replacement shipping at no additional charge, at agent discretion, without requiring proof of the stated reason.

## 5. International Shipping

International orders follow the same lost/damaged remedies as domestic orders, but replacement shipments may take longer due to customs processing; set expectations accordingly and offer store credit as an alternative under stored_value §1 if the customer cannot wait for a second international shipment. Customs duty on a replacement shipment is borne by us, never re-charged to the customer for a loss or defect that was ours to remedy.

## 6. Serviceable Pincodes and Remote Areas

Delivery windows in §1 assume a serviceable pincode. Where the delivery pincode has a longer standard transit time already disclosed at checkout, that disclosed time is the baseline for "late" under §1, not the general window. Where a pincode becomes temporarily unserviceable after an order is placed (a courier hub disruption, a regional restriction), the order-lifecycle and communication obligations in orders §7 and comms §5 apply — the customer is told promptly and given the choice to wait, switch to an alternative address, or cancel for a full refund.
