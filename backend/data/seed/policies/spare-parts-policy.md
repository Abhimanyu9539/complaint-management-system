# Spare Parts & Accessories Policy

This policy governs replacement parts and accessories sold as SKUs on our own store: when a part is free, when it is a paid order, how long we keep making it available, and which parts must be fitted by the authorized service network rather than the customer.

## 1. Wear Is Not a Defect

Components that wear out through normal use — blades dulling, filters loading, seals hardening, brushes shortening, batteries losing capacity gradually over years — are consumables, and replacing them is a paid order at any age. A component that fails rather than wears, in a way and at a time inconsistent with its expected life, is a defect and is handled under warranty §2 instead. The distinguishing question is not the age of the unit but the manner of failure: blades worn down after eighteen months of daily use are wear, and a blade assembly that cracked in five months is not. Where the answer is genuinely unclear, treat it as a defect and let the returned part settle it.

## 2. Parts Under Warranty Are Free, Including Delivery

Where a part is being replaced because of a covered defect, the part, its shipping and any prepaid reverse pickup for the failed part are all free, and the customer is never asked to pay and claim it back. Support should not route a warranty part through the paid parts flow for administrative convenience — a customer being asked to pay on a warranty claim is a policy failure regardless of whether the money is later refunded. Fitment of a warranty part is arranged through the service ladder in warranty §7, not sent to the customer to self-install.

## 3. Availability After Discontinuation

Functional parts are stocked for at least **5 years** after a product line is discontinued, and cosmetic or packaging parts are not guaranteed beyond it. Where a needed part is unavailable within that window, the customer is entitled to a remedy rather than an apology: a compatible current-generation part where one exists, or a discount against the current equivalent product where none does. Where the part is unavailable outside that window, say so plainly and offer the current-generation discount as a courtesy — the honest answer given quickly is worth more than an open order that never ships.

## 4. Pricing and Lead Time

Parts are sold at listed price plus standard shipping, and the lead time quoted is the standard delivery window in shipping §1 measured from dispatch, not from order. Where a part is on back-order, the customer is given the expected dispatch date before they pay, not after, and is told they may cancel for a full refund if the date moves. Expedited shipping on a paid part is available at the customer's cost, except where the part order results from our error or a covered defect, in which case shipping §4 applies at no charge.

## 5. Verify Compatibility Before Taking the Order

The model, generation and serial number are checked against the part before the order is placed, because most wrong-part returns are our specification error rather than the customer's mistake. If the customer cannot supply the serial, use the original order ID. Where a wrong part is shipped against a correctly supplied serial, that is company error: the replacement ships immediately, the reverse pickup is prepaid, and the full amount including original shipping is refunded per returns §4. Where a customer ordered against their own specification without verification, standard return handling applies.

## 6. Parts That Must Be Fitted by the Service Network

Some parts are not customer-installable regardless of the customer's confidence or skill: mains-connected assemblies, battery packs and charging circuits, sealed motor units, and anything whose removal defeats a safety interlock. These are supplied only through the authorized service network under warranty §7, not sold as a standalone SKU, and a customer asking to buy one directly is told why, not simply declined. Where a customer reports that they have already fitted such a part themselves, treat the unit as modified for warranty purposes under warranty §3, and if they report any safety symptom afterwards, product_safety §1 applies immediately and without argument about who fitted what.

## 7. Returns of Parts

Individually ordered parts are not returnable once fitted or once their sealed packaging is opened, consistent with returns §5, because a part that has been installed cannot be resold and its condition cannot be verified. Unfitted parts in unopened packaging may be returned inside the standard window in returns §1, through the reverse-pickup process in reverse §1. This restriction never limits a defect or safety remedy: a part that arrives faulty, is the wrong part, or turns out to be unsafe is remedied under warranty or product_safety whatever its packaging state.
