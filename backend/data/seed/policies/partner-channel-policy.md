# Partner & Marketplace Channel Policy

This policy is company-wide and governs complaints from two different kinds of counterparty: retail and wholesale partners who buy our stock to resell, and the marketplace platforms (e-commerce marketplaces we also list on, distinct from our own site and app) where a consumer bought our product but is writing to us directly. Both are outside our own storefront; both still end with our warranty applying to the end customer.

## 1. Who the Counterparty Is

On a wholesale claim the partner is our customer, and the commercial remedy is negotiated with them. On a marketplace-channel complaint, there is no separate commercial counterparty in the ticket at all — the consumer bought through another platform's checkout, but the product, the warranty and the remedy are still ours. Either way, **a consumer who bought our product anywhere has the same warranty we would owe them directly**, running from the invoice date on their order per warranty §1, and they may claim from us without going back through the partner or the marketplace. A consumer must never be sent back and forth between us and a channel; whoever they reach first owns the remedy, and we settle the commercial side, if any, afterwards.

## 2. Claim Windows for Wholesale Shipments

Shortages, external damage and count discrepancies must be reported within **10 business days** of receipt, because after that we cannot distinguish transit damage from warehouse handling. Concealed manufacturing defects — the kind found only on unboxing for display, or reported later by consumers — may be claimed at any point within the product's warranty window, since a partner cannot be expected to open every unit on arrival. Where a partner reports a defect after selling stock on, the claim covers the units they can account for.

## 3. One Unit or the Batch

A single failure in a wholesale shipment is handled as an individual warranty claim. **Multiple failures sharing a defect and a batch are a batch claim**, and are routed to manufacturing §1 with the shipment reference and affected unit count — twelve failures in fifty units is a production signal, not twelve warranty claims, and handling it as the latter loses the signal entirely. Partner batch claims are `high` severity on arrival (§6), and the containment decision belongs to manufacturing §3, not to the commercial conversation.

## 4. Commercial Remedies

The remedy for a confirmed batch defect affecting a partner is **replacement of the affected units from stock verified to be outside the affected batch** (manufacturing §6), plus a **disruption credit of up to 5% of the order value** for the handling and lost selling time. Credits above 5% are an admin decision (authority §2). Replacement units ship without waiting for the defective stock to come back; the return is arranged separately and at our cost. Credit is applied against the order, not paid as cash, unless the partner relationship is ending.

## 5. Quarantined Stock and Shelf Recovery

Where stock is quarantined under manufacturing §3, a wholesale partner receives **written** instructions: which batch, what to stop selling, how to segregate it, and what happens next. Verbal instructions on a partner call are not sufficient — the person who takes the call is rarely the person who picks the stock. Partners must not return quarantined units into circulation, discount them to clear them, or ship them onward, and must not decide for themselves what to do with stock we have asked them to hold. Where units from a quarantined batch have already reached consumers through the partner, whether and how those consumers are contacted is a Product Safety and Legal decision (manufacturing §4).

## 6. Response Expectations

Wholesale partner claims are treated as `high` severity by default under severity §1 — a partner with unsellable stock is blocked in the way that definition describes — and follow the targets in sla §2 and §3 accordingly. A marketplace-channel complaint from a consumer follows the same severity assessment as any other consumer complaint reaching us directly (severity §1–§2); it is not automatically lower priority for having arrived through another platform. Each wholesale partner account has a named contact on our side, and claims are not worked through the general queue where that contact exists. A partner or marketplace batch report involving any safety symptom is critical and follows product_safety §1 immediately, ahead of the commercial discussion.

## 7. Neither Partners Nor Marketplace Listings Speak for Us

A partner may tell their customers what remedy is available and how to reach us. Neither a partner nor a marketplace listing's seller-support text may state our policy beyond what we have given them in writing, characterise a defect's cause, use recall language (product_safety §3.1), or make statements to a regulator, a consumer commission or the press on our behalf (legal §6). Where a partner or a marketplace listing has already told a consumer something incorrect, we honour what the consumer was told if it is within reach and correct it with the channel afterwards — the consumer is not made to argue about who said what, in the same way sales §7 handles a commitment made in error.

## 8. Pricing, Rebates and Commercial Terms

Volume pricing, rebate calculations, and quote validity for wholesale partners are commercial questions handled under sales §5 — not complaint remedies, and not resolvable from the support queue. Marketplace-channel pricing (a listing price on another platform differing from ours) is not a pricing dispute we owe a remedy on unless the consumer paid us directly for something different from what they received; where the complaint is genuinely about our product or our fulfilment, it is handled exactly as it would be for a direct order. Where a partner raises a pricing dispute alongside a defect claim, the two are separated: the defect remedy proceeds under §4 without waiting for the commercial discussion to conclude.
